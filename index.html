Not sure which you need?

Most projects need two separate drawing packages.

Planning drawings establish whether you are allowed to build what you want. They show the design, the scale and the relationship to neighbouring property, and they go to your local planning authority.

Building regulations drawings establish how it is built. They cover structure, insulation, ventilation, drainage and fire safety, and they go to building control. They are also the set your builder prices from.

Planning permission does not give you building regulations approval, and vice versa. Tell us about your project and we will tell you which you need — and whether you need both.

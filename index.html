FAQs

Answers before your project starts

The questions we are asked most often by homeowners, landlords and developers.

What architectural design services do you provide across the UK?

We provide planning drawings, house extension drawings, loft conversion drawings, HMO and flat conversion drawings, building regulations packages, new build design, Land Registry lease plans, drop kerb drawings and outbuilding drawings for clients throughout the United Kingdom.

Can you prepare planning drawings for a house extension?

Yes. We produce existing and proposed floor plans, elevations, roof plans, site plans at 1:200 and location plans at 1:1250 — the full set a householder planning application needs to validate first time.

Do you provide building regulations drawings as well?

Yes. Building regulations packages include dimensioned construction plans, sections, U-value and ventilation schedules, drainage layouts and fire safety information prepared for a full plans application.

Do you work with homeowners as well as professional property teams?

Both. We work with homeowners, self-builders, landlords, developers, builders, planning consultants, architects and property managers, adjusting the level of technical detail to suit the client.

How quickly can my drawings be completed?

A typical householder planning set takes one to two weeks from survey, with building regulations drawings taking a similar period afterwards. We confirm a programme in writing before starting.

How much do architectural drawings cost?

We quote a fixed price for a clearly defined scope after a short call, rather than working on open-ended hourly rates. The fee depends on property size, project type and whether you need planning drawings, building regulations drawings or both.

Do you work remotely or do you visit the property?

Most projects are delivered remotely using measured information, existing drawings and photographic surveys, which keeps costs down. Where a full measured survey is required we will say so at the outset and agree it before starting.

How Much Does an Architect Cost for a House Extension?

Understand architect fees for UK house extensions, including fixed fees, hourly rates and percentage fees, plus lower-cost drawing-only options.

Design & drawings7 September 2026By UK CAD Studio Editorial Team9 min read
Quick answer: UK architect fees for an extension can be charged as a fixed sum, hourly rate or percentage of construction cost. Published 2026 guides suggest roughly 3%–15% of build cost for different service levels, while planning-only drawing packages for a simple extension may be around £1,000–£2,500.

Updated for 2026. This guide is written in simple terms for homeowners, landlords and residential developers. Planning rules quoted here relate mainly to England. Always check the exact property and local authority requirements before starting work.

How architects charge

RIBA describes three common fee methods: a percentage of construction cost, a lump sum, or time charges. The right method depends on how clearly the project scope is defined and how much support you want through design and construction.

A full architectural service is very different from buying a planning drawing package. Full service can include briefing, concept design, planning, technical design, tender support, contract administration and site involvement.

Planning-only fees

For homeowners who already know broadly what they want, a fixed planning package can be easier to budget. Current market guides show simple single-storey extension planning work around £1,000–£2,500, with more complex or two-storey projects costing more.

Check whether the measured survey, design revisions and submission handling are included before comparing prices.

Technical design and construction support

Building Regulations and construction drawings add technical coordination. If the architect also manages tendering, builder queries and work on site, fees increase because the service continues for much longer.

Percentage fees can make sense for a full service because the time and responsibility usually rise with construction value and complexity.

When an architectural designer may suit you

You do not always need a full architect-led service for a straightforward domestic extension. An experienced architectural designer or technician can prepare planning and technical CAD packages where that matches the project needs.

For unusual sites, listed buildings, highly design-led projects or complex procurement, broader architectural input may provide more value.

Ask to see relevant residential examples and confirm exactly what will be delivered. Clear drawings can reduce ambiguity when builders quote and help avoid expensive changes later.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage do architects charge?

Published UK guidance commonly shows a broad range around 3%–15% of construction cost depending on project size, complexity and service level.

Can an architect charge a fixed fee?

Yes. Fixed fees are common where the project and deliverables can be defined clearly.

Do I need an architect for a house extension?

There is no general legal requirement to appoint an architect for a normal house extension, but you do need competent design and technical information.

Is an architectural designer cheaper?

Often, particularly for drawing-focused packages, but compare experience and scope rather than price alone.

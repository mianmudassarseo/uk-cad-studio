FAQs

Before you get in touch

A few things clients usually want to know first.

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

We reply to every enquiry within one working day, and usually the same day if you contact us before mid-afternoon. If your project is time-critical, say so in your message and we will prioritise it.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

The property address or postcode, the type of project, whether you have any existing drawings or a title plan, and your preferred timing. If you have photographs of the property, mention it and we will send a secure way to share them.

Do you charge for an initial consultation?

No. The initial call and project review are free. We will tell you what drawings you need, what the likely programme is, and give you a fixed-fee quote before you commit to anything.

Do you need to visit my property?

Usually not. Most projects are delivered remotely from measured information, existing drawings and photographs. Where a full measured survey is genuinely needed we will say so upfront and agree it separately.

Can you work with my builder or architect?

Yes. We frequently work alongside contractors, structural engineers, planning consultants and architects, and can issue drawings in the formats and file types your wider team needs.

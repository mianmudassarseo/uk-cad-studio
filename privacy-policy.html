Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you give us, and what your rights are under UK data protection law.

Last updated: 07 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data UKCADStudio collects through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We are the data controller for that information.

What we collect

When you complete a form on this site we collect the information you enter: your name, email address, telephone number, project postcode, the service you have selected and the details of your enquiry. Our server also records your IP address and the time of submission, which we use to prevent spam and abuse.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on this site. We do load web fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you view a page.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide a quotation — our lawful basis is that processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To deliver a project you have engaged us for — necessary for the performance of a contract.
  • To prevent spam and protect the website — our legitimate interests.
  • To send occasional insight emails, where you have subscribed — consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. Your information is stored on our email and hosting providers' systems as part of normal operation. Where a project requires it, we may share relevant project details with consultants you have asked us to coordinate with — we will tell you before we do.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 24 months. Project records are kept for six years after completion, which reflects the period during which a contractual claim could be brought.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to request erasure, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email raotipusultaan@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

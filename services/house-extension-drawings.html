A successful extension starts with clear drawing plans. Accurate existing and proposed plans help homeowners understand the layout, give planning officers the information they need to assess a proposal and help builders review the intended work. The right drawings depend on your property, the proposed changes and whether planning permission or building regulations approval is required.

UKCADStudio prepares house extension drawings for homeowners, builders and developers through a UK-wide remote drawing service. We work from suitable existing plans, measurements, sketches, photographs and survey information supplied for the project. If an on-site measured survey is needed, it must be arranged separately and its scope confirmed before the drawings begin.

How to get drawing plans for your extension

Architectural drawings for an extension may also include construction-level information, but planning drawings and building regulations drawings are not interchangeable. If you need both stages, we can discuss the deliverables separately so you know what is included before work begins.

Typical extension planning drawings show the existing house and the proposed works clearly enough for the planning authority to assess the changes. Depending on the submission, these may include existing and proposed floor plans, elevations, roof plans, a location plan and a site or block plan. Additional sections or supporting information may be requested for more complex proposals.

Extension types we draw

Single-storey rear extensions — the most common project we handle. The design work is usually about daylight: how far you can go back before the middle of the plan turns dark, and whether rooflights, a glazed link or a raised ceiling solve it.

Side-return extensions — filling in the narrow alley beside a Victorian or Edwardian terrace. Small in footprint, disproportionately large in effect, and technically fiddly where the new structure meets the existing flank wall and the neighbour's boundary.

Two-storey and first-floor extensions — where the constraint moves from daylight to massing, roof form and the 45-degree impact on neighbouring windows. These almost always need full planning permission.

Wrap-around extensions — a rear and side extension combined. The best value per pound of construction, but the drawings have to resolve a complicated roof junction and often a new steel frame.

Porches, garage conversions and infills — smaller works that still need accurate drawings for building control, warranties and future sale.

What we produce

We prepare drawing plans for an extension according to the agreed brief. A planning drawing pack may include existing and proposed floor plans, elevations, roof plans and the relevant site and location plans. A separate building regulations drawings pack can include construction plans, sections, build-up details and specification notes where required. The exact plans, scales and specialist inputs depend on the application and project, and are confirmed in your quotation.

Planning drawings explain the proposed size, position and appearance of an extension. Building regulations drawings address how the works are to be constructed and assessed for compliance. Structural engineering calculations, energy assessments, application fees and site surveys are separate unless expressly included in the agreed scope.

Permitted development or full planning?

Some single-storey rear extensions in England may fall within permitted development rights, depending on the property and proposal. Even where planning permission is not required, building regulations approval may still be needed. For more detail, read our permitted development vs planning permission guide.

The allowances depend on whether your house is detached or attached, how high the extension is, how close it comes to a boundary, and whether the property sits in a conservation area, a National Park or an Article 4 area. Flats and maisonettes have no permitted development rights at all, and rights are frequently removed on newer estates by planning condition.

We review the planning information available for your project and highlight questions to confirm with the relevant local planning authority. Restrictions and permitted development rules differ across the UK, so eligibility should not be assumed without checking the property and its location.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we are asked most often.

What drawings do I need for a house extension?

For a planning application, drawings may include existing and proposed floor plans, elevations, a roof plan, and appropriate site and location plans. A separate building regulations package may be required for construction details. The exact set and drawing scales depend on the project and the local authority’s requirements.

How much do house extension drawings cost in the UK?

Costs depend on property size, drawing complexity, the accuracy of information supplied and whether you need planning drawings, building regulations drawings or both. We provide a fixed quote for an agreed scope, including specified deliverables and revisions.

How long does it take to get extension drawings?

Timescales depend on project complexity, the survey information available, the agreed drawing package and the speed of feedback. We confirm an estimated delivery schedule with the quotation rather than promising the same turnaround for every project.

Do I need planning permission for a rear extension?

Not always. Some extensions to houses may qualify as permitted development, subject to limits and conditions. Rules vary across the UK and can be restricted by the property’s planning history or location. Building regulations approval is a separate consideration.

Can you draw an extension if I already have an architect's sketch?

Yes. We can review your sketch, existing floor plan and measurements to determine whether there is enough information to prepare accurate drawings. If further measurements or a measured survey are necessary, we will flag that before proceeding.

Do you deal with the council on my behalf?

We can prepare planning submission drawings. Application submission, council correspondence and revisions requested by the authority are included only where specifically agreed in the quotation.

Can you prepare extension drawings remotely?

Yes. Our drawing service is delivered remotely using suitable plans, measurements, photographs and project details. If accurate on-site measurements are required, a survey must be arranged separately before the drawings can be finalised.

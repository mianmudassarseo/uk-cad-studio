About UKCADStudio

Practical architectural thinking. Precise technical delivery.

A residential CAD and design studio working with homeowners, landlords, builders and developers across the United Kingdom.

Get a free project reviewCall +92 305 6205832

UKCADStudio exists because a great many residential projects are delayed, over-priced or refused for reasons that have nothing to do with the quality of the idea. They fail because the survey was approximate, the submission was incomplete, or the technical package was too vague for a builder to price properly.

We produce the drawings that stop those things happening. Measured surveys, planning submissions that validate first time, and building regulations packages detailed enough that three contractors quoting from them produce three comparable numbers.

How we work

We are a remote-first studio. That is a deliberate choice rather than a compromise: it lets us work across the whole of the UK, keeps our overheads — and therefore our fees — lower than a high-street practice, and means most clients get their drawings faster.

In practice, a project starts with a short call about the property and what you want to achieve. We check the planning constraints for the address, confirm what drawings you actually need, and quote a fixed fee for a defined scope. Nothing starts until you have that in writing.

What we believe about drawings

Accuracy beats presentation. A beautifully rendered visual that is 200mm out on a boundary is worse than useless. Every drawing we issue is built on measured information, and where we are working from client-supplied dimensions we say so on the drawing.

Say what you know and flag what you do not. If a scheme is marginal on planning grounds, we will tell you before you pay for it. If we think a specialist heritage or planning consultant would materially improve your chances, we will say that too, even though it means recommending work we do not do.

Scope should be fixed, not discovered. Open-ended hourly billing on residential drawing work rewards the wrong things. We quote a fixed fee for a defined deliverable and include an agreed number of revision rounds in it.

Who we work with

Roughly half of our work is for homeowners extending, converting or reconfiguring a house they live in. The rest is for landlords and small developers — HMO and flat conversions, infill plots and small residential schemes — plus a steady stream of lease plan work for conveyancers and managing agents.

We also work as an outsourced drawing resource for builders, planning consultants and architectural practices who need overflow capacity on residential packages.

Our standards

Consistency is the point.

Every project runs through the same process, with the same CAD standards and the same version control, whether it is a single lease plan or a nine-bedroom HMO conversion.
